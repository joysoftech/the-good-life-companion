Beyond the Pages: Your Interactive Companion to The Good Life

“The question is not only what makes a life good, but what kind of life we are choosing to become.”
Reading about ethics can sharpen the mind, yet real moral understanding happens when an idea interrupts our plans and asks us to choose under pressure.
Alongside this book, I have prepared a personal, interactive ethical simulator, The Good Life: Interactive Reader Companion, designed to be played one chapter at a time as you read. Rather than testing how well you memorize definitions, this companion places you inside real-world dilemmas where time, resources, and clarity are limited, and every choice carries a quiet cost.

How to Begin

1.	Scan the QR Code below with your smartphone camera (or open the web link on your tablet or laptop).
2.	Enter Your Name on the opening screen so your personal reflections and ethical scorecard are tailored to you.
3.	Read First, Then Play: Complete Chapter 1 in this book, then open Chapter 1 in the companion. Each subsequent chapter unlocks sequentially as you complete the one before it—or, if you have already finished the book or are using this in a classroom seminar, switch to Classroom / Advanced Mode to open all six chapters at once.
 
How Each Chapter Episode Works

•	Three Interlocking Cases: Every chapter immerses you in three distinct scenarios—an everyday personal decision, a civic or workplace dilemma, and an encounter drawn directly from the city square of this book with Plato, Aristotle, and Jesus.

•	The 20T Wisdom Budget: You begin each chapter with 20 Wisdom Tokens (20T) across three cases that would require 30T to investigate completely. You can spend 5T to HEAR the hidden human reality beneath a conflict, or 5T to SEARCH for deeper ethical and scriptural principles. Because your tokens are scarce, you must decide where deeper inquiry is most urgent—and how to rule when certainty runs out.

•	Your Chapter Ethical Autopsy: At the end of each chapter, you will receive an Ethical Autopsy measuring how your rulings balanced Justice, Mercy, and Community, followed by a personal Mirror Check and a preview leading into the next chapter.


•	Your Personal Dossier: Your progress saves automatically on your device. Upon completing all six chapters, you can download or copy your complete Personal Ethical Autopsy & Introspection Dossier as a keepsake of your journey.

Take your time. Sit at the gate, weigh the competing claims, and discover not only what you believe about the good life—but the kind of person your choices are forming you to be.

— Anand S. Deshbhratar
